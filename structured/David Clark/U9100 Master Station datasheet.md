## Model U9100 Master Station

On land or at sea; for facilities or mobile platforms; in harsh, noisy environments or in quiet areas over long distances; for single or multi-channel communication; with wired security and wireless mobility, the Series 9100 Digital     Intercom       System      provides

### communication clarity for the working world.

The U9100 Master Station is the heart of the digital system, providing high performance Ethernet/IP versatility and a modular approach to system interface connectivity to two-way radios and other common ancillaries, as well as existing networks.

The result is a configuration scheme highly adaptable to a broad spectrum of diverse applications, and a communication system that is at once both highly reliable and versatile, yet
